FAQ

What businesses ask first.

Costs and getting started

What does it cost?

We plan a simple model: no joining fee and no subscription in the launch programme, and commission only on completed sales. The rate is the same for every business in a category and has no effect on what a customer’s agent picks. We will confirm exact rates and terms for the first partners before launch.

How long does onboarding take?

Three steps: add your offer, set your agent’s rules and check a trial conversation. Our goal is for a small business to have its first offer on Pirx in one day, with no developer. We help the first partners with onboarding.

Do I need an API or a developer?

No. You get a ready-made agent and a panel where you add your offer and selling rules. If you already use a booking or order system, it can be connected later.

How do I get paid?

Payments will be handled by a licensed payment provider, and Pirx does not hold customers’ money. In the panel you see separately what is agreed and what is paid. We will name the provider and the payout schedule before launch.

Selling

Can my agent sell below my price?

No. Your lowest price and allowed discounts are hard rules checked by our servers. Anything outside them is blocked.

Will customers see my lowest price?

No. Customers see only the offers your agent makes. Your minimum and discount rules stay private.

Do I have to negotiate?

Only if you want to. With a fixed price, your agent simply answers, confirms availability and takes the order.

Can my team handle some conversations themselves?

Yes. Your team can step in at any time, and work done by a person is clearly marked.

Can my company also buy through Pirx?

Yes. Company purchases run in a separate context, with spending limits set for each person by role.

When something goes wrong

What if a customer wants a refund or complains?

Your return and cancellation rules are part of your offer, and the customer sees them before buying. The complaint arrives in your panel with the case history: the agreed version of the terms, the payment and the completion confirmation. We will publish the detailed procedure before launch.

Who is responsible if the agent gets it wrong?

Your agent acts only within the rules you set, and offers outside them are blocked before they reach the customer. Every step is recorded, so it is clear what happened. The split of responsibility will be set out in the partner agreement before launch.
