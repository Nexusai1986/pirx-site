4. Cookies and tracking

This website does not use advertising cookies or third-party analytics, and it does not load fonts or scripts from other companies’ servers. If we add analytics later, we will update this policy first and ask for your consent where the law requires it.

5. Legal basis

We use your data on the basis of your consent, which you give by signing up, and on the basis of our legitimate interest in running and protecting the website. We follow the UAE Federal Decree-Law No. 45 of 2021 on the Protection of Personal Data. Where the EU General Data Protection Regulation applies to you, the bases are Article 6(1)(a) and Article 6(1)(f).

6. What we do not do

  • We do not sell your data.
  • We do not use it for advertising profiles.
  • We do not send messages unrelated to Pirx.

7. Who receives your data

We share data only with service providers who work for us under a contract, such as a hosting provider and an email service, and with authorities where the law requires it. We will list the providers here before the sign-up forms are switched on.

8. Transfers outside the UAE

Some providers may process data outside the United Arab Emirates. Where they do, we rely on the safeguards required by applicable law, such as contractual protections.

9. How long we keep it

We keep waitlist and partner data until you unsubscribe or ask us to delete it. If Pirx has not opened in your area within 24 months of your sign-up, we delete your data or ask whether you want to stay on the list. Server logs are kept for a short period needed for security.

10. Your rights

You can ask us to show you your data, correct it, delete it, limit how we use it or send it to you in a portable form. You can withdraw your consent and unsubscribe at any time; every email from us will include a link to do so. To use these rights, write to [contact email to be added before publication]. You may also complain to the UAE Data Office or, where the GDPR applies, to your local data protection authority.

11. Children

This website is not directed at people under 18, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

12. Security

We limit access to the data to people who need it and use providers that apply appropriate technical and organisational protection. No system is perfectly secure, so we collect as little as we need.

13. Changes

If we change this policy, we will publish the new version here with a new date. If the change is significant, we will tell people on the lists by email.
