FAQ

Questions people ask first.

Money and safety

What does it cost?

We plan for Pirx to be free for customers during early access. If a fee is introduced later, you will see it before you approve anything and it will count towards your limit. The commission paid by businesses does not raise your price and has no effect on which offer your agent picks.

How do I pay, and is my card safe?

Payments will be handled by a licensed payment provider. Pirx does not hold your money. Your agent can pay only when you switch payment on, and only up to your limit, and you get confirmation of each payment. We will name the provider and the available payment methods before launch.

Can it spend more than I allow?

No. The limit is a hard rule, not a target. If no offer fits, your agent buys nothing and tells you what it found, with options: raise the limit, change the request or search longer.

Will businesses see my budget?

No. Your limit stays private. The business only sees your request and your agent’s offers.

When something goes wrong

What if the agent buys the wrong thing?

Before searching, your agent confirms your request as “must have, nice to have, doesn’t matter”. It compares only offers that match the must-haves, for example exactly your model and size. If anything is unclear, it asks you instead of guessing.

How do returns and complaints work?

Your contract is with the business, so its return and cancellation terms apply. Your agent checks them and shows them to you before buying. The agreed terms, the payment and the delivery or completion confirmation stay in the case history, so you raise a complaint from the app with the full record. We will publish the detailed procedure before launch.

What if there’s a dispute with the business?

Both sides see the same recorded version of the terms, so there is no argument about what was agreed. You raise the issue in the app, and the business and our team see the same history. The dispute rules will be part of the service terms, which we will publish before launch.

Who is responsible if the agent gets it wrong?

Your agent acts only within the limits you approve, and each step is checked and recorded. If something goes against your rules, report it: the history shows what happened and on whose side. The exact split of responsibility between Pirx, the business and the customer will be set out in the service terms before launch.

Can I stop it halfway?

Yes, at any time. Future steps stop at once. Anything already agreed stays on record, so you know where you stand.

How it works

Where do I talk to my agent?

In the Pirx app. At launch it runs in your browser, on phone and desktop, and iOS and Android apps are the next step. You type like in any chat; voice will follow after launch. When your agent needs your decision, you get an in-app notification.

Where does it look for offers?

Among businesses that have joined Pirx and systems connected to it. At launch we focus on a few categories in Dubai: home services, appointments and stores. Your agent always shows how many offers it actually checked.

Can I use it for my company too?

Yes. Personal and company purchases run in separate contexts with separate limits. See Pirx for business.

When can I start?

Pirx is being built and will launch in Dubai. Join the waitlist and we’ll let you know when your access is ready. We are not announcing a launch date yet.
